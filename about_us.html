Our Story

Established in 2048, The Cosmic Horizon became the world's first luxury orbital resort. Located 400 km above Earth, we combine cutting-edge technology such as hydroponic systems with five-star comfort, allowing guests to witness sixteen sunrises every day while enjoying unforgettable experiences in space.

🚀 Our Mission

To provide extraordinary, safe and sustainable luxury experiences beyond Earth.

🌌 Our Vision

To make space tourism the next generation of travel.
