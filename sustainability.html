Building a Sustainable Future in Space

At Cosmic Horizon, innovation and environmental responsibility work together to create unforgettable experiences beyond Earth.

Our Mission

Every part of Cosmic Horizon has been designed to reduce waste, conserve resources and protect both our planet and the space environment. Through cutting-edge technology, renewable energy and sustainable farming, we aim to make luxury space tourism environmentally responsible.

🌱 Hydroponic Farming

Fresh vegetables are grown aboard the hotel using advanced hydroponic systems that require 90% less water than traditional farming.

☀ Solar Energy

High-efficiency solar panels capture energy directly from the Sun, powering the hotel with clean renewable electricity.

💧 Water Recycling

Our advanced filtration systems recycle almost every drop of water used onboard, reducing waste and preserving valuable resources.

🛰 Efficient Design

Artificial gravity is created through controlled rotation, reducing energy consumption while maximising guest comfort.
